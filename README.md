I am an AI and backend engineer with over 10 years of experience designing and successfully building scalable web applications, AI powered products, and enterprise grade software solutions. Leveraging expertise in React, Next.js, TypeScript, and modern frontend architectures, I build backend systems, AI-powered applications, and distributed services with a focus on scalability, reliability, and maintainability.
